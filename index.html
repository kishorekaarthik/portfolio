CyberVault

Web app, the digital safe

A vault for secrets that actually stays shut. Everything is AES-encrypted, you prove it’s you with a one-time code or Google sign-in, JWT keeps the session honest, and role-based access decides who gets into which room. Splunk watches the doors and Docker ships the whole building. Built on Flask and MongoDB.

Flask, MongoDB, AES, OAuth2, JWT, RBAC, Splunk, Docker

CyberShield

Machine learning, with a paper to prove it

An AI that reads the room, words and pictures together, so a nasty meme can’t hide behind a harmless caption. DistilBERT handles the text, ResNet-18 the images, trained on MMHS150K. There’s a Flask API, a Chrome extension, and a research paper written with Kiruthikrokan B.

DistilBERT, ResNet-18, Flask, Chrome extension
